# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name

**ScreenBased**

## 1. Product Overview

**ScreenBased** is a **digital queue and reservation management system** designed for environments where people line up for services (e.g., **school accounting, registrar, clinics, government offices**).

Instead of physically lining up, users can **reserve a queue slot digitally and monitor their position through a screen display system**.

The goal is to **reduce waiting time, eliminate long physical lines, and improve service efficiency**.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

In many schools and offices:

* Students line up physically for **accounting payments, document requests, or verification**
* Long queues cause **crowding and wasted time**
* Staff struggle to **manage and monitor queue flow**
* Students often **don’t know how long they will wait**

**Current Situation Example (School Accounting):**

1. Students arrive
2. They form a long line
3. No clear queue tracking
4. Some people cut lines
5. Staff manually calls the next person

This creates **frustration and inefficiency**.

---

# 3. Product Vision

To create a **smart queue management system that organizes service flow through digital screens and online reservations**.

Users should be able to:

* Reserve a slot
* See their queue number
* Monitor progress on a display screen
* Arrive only when their number is close

---

# 4. Target Users

### Primary Users

Students or customers who want to reserve a queue.

### Secondary Users

Office staff (accounting personnel).

### Admin Users

Administrators who manage queue flow and services.

---

# 5. Core Features

## 5.1 Queue Reservation

Users can reserve a queue slot before arriving.

**Capabilities:**

* Select service (Accounting, Registrar, etc.)
* Generate queue number
* Show estimated waiting time

---

## 5.2 Live Queue Tracking

Users can monitor the queue progress.

**Features:**

* Current number being served
* Numbers waiting
* Estimated time

Example:

```
Now Serving: A023
Next: A024
Your Number: A030
Estimated Wait: 15 minutes
```

---

## 5.3 Display Screen System

A **public screen displays queue progress**.

This can be placed in:

* Accounting offices
* Waiting areas
* School lobbies

The screen shows:

* Current serving number
* Next numbers
* Announcements

---

## 5.4 Staff Queue Controls

Staff can control queue flow.

Actions include:

* Call next number
* Skip number
* Mark as completed
* Pause queue

---

## 5.5 Service Categories

Multiple service queues can exist simultaneously.

Example:

| Service       | Prefix |
| ------------- | ------ |
| Accounting    | A      |
| Registrar     | R      |
| ID Processing | I      |

Queue numbers look like:

* A021
* R015
* I008

---

## 5.6 Notifications

Users receive updates when their turn is near.

Possible channels:

* Web notifications
* SMS (future)
* Email (future)

Example:

> “Your queue number A030 is now approaching. Please proceed to Accounting.”

3. Call next number

## Display Screen Flow

1. System shows active queues
2. Automatically updates when staff calls next
